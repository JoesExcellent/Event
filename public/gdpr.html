GDPR & Privacy Policy

Joe's Excellent Events & Management is committed to protecting personal information and handling candidate, client, employee and business partner data responsibly.

Privacy, Trust and Data Protection

Joe's Excellent Events & Management respects the privacy of every person who uses our services, applies for opportunities, works with us, or shares personal information with us. We understand that protecting personal data is essential to maintaining trust, professionalism and confidence.

This policy explains how we aim to collect, use, store and protect personal information in connection with recruitment, candidate management, client services, onboarding, employment support and related business activities.

Information We May Collect

We may collect personal information that is necessary to provide recruitment, employment, client support and management services.

  • Name and contact details.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Application information submitted through our website.
  • CV, work history, skills, qualifications and availability.
  • Interview details, recruitment status and onboarding information.
  • Right to work, training, DBS or employment-readiness information where relevant.
  • Client and business contact information required for professional services.

How We Use Personal Information

Personal information is used only where necessary and appropriate for legitimate recruitment, employment, onboarding, client support and business administration purposes.

  • To process applications and manage recruitment records.
  • To contact candidates about applications, interviews and onboarding.
  • To support client services and professional communication.
  • To assess suitability for roles, placements, training or employment opportunities.
  • To manage candidate portal access and recruitment progress.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, safeguarding or employment obligations where applicable.

Candidate Portal Privacy

The Candidate Portal is designed to help candidates view their recruitment progress securely. Application references displayed within the portal may be partially masked to reduce the risk of unauthorised disclosure, especially where a candidate may be accessing the portal in a public place.

Candidates should keep their application reference and login details private and should log out after using the portal, particularly on shared or public devices.

Data Security

We aim to protect personal information using appropriate administrative, technical and organisational safeguards. Access to candidate and client information is restricted to authorised personnel who need access to perform their duties.

We do not knowingly share personal information with unauthorised third parties. Where information needs to be shared for recruitment, employment, client service, legal or operational reasons, this will be handled responsibly and only where there is a lawful basis to do so.

Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary for recruitment, onboarding, employment, client service, legal, safeguarding, audit or business administration purposes.

When personal information is no longer required, it should be reviewed and securely deleted, archived or anonymised in accordance with appropriate data retention procedures.

Your Rights

Individuals may have the right to request access to their personal information, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, raise concerns about how their information is being used, or request deletion where applicable.

Requests will be reviewed carefully and handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

Client and Business Partner Information

Joe's Excellent Events & Management also recognises the importance of protecting client and business partner information. Business contact details, project information and professional communications are handled with care, confidentiality and respect.

Contact Us

For GDPR enquiries, privacy requests, correction requests or data protection concerns, please contact Joe's Excellent Events & Management using the contact information provided on this website.
